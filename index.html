Profile

Nigel
Hodgson

Every department in a live show has a designer — lighting, sound, set. Automation and systems integration rarely gets the same recognition, but for over 35 years, that's been my role: translating artistic intent into technical scope across animatronic show control, multi-protocol AV networking, IT infrastructure, and technical operations management — and translating technical constraint back into creative options, so producers get the best achievable outcome rather than a forced compromise. Across touring productions, permanent installations, and travelling exhibitions on every continent.

I have served as Head of Show Control, Head of Creatures, Technical Supervisor, and IT Coordinator on productions that run seven days a week, move to a new venue every week, and cannot fail. That operating reality — not classroom theory — informs every engagement I take on.

Based between Sydney and Osaka, I am positioned across the Asia Pacific region and available for remote consultations, on-site animatronic commissioning support, exhibition operations management, and retained technical advisory for exhibition companies, production houses, and themed entertainment operators.

Commissioning & Technical Readiness

When you need it
An animatronic, show control system or exhibition is being installed or tested and needs to reach reliable, repeatable operation.
What I provide
Commissioning support and testing coordination within agreed scope, bench testing and pre-show verification, fault diagnosis and troubleshooting across automation, AV and IT, and readiness checks before opening. Experienced across Alcorn McBride, Medialon, Beckhoff, CANbus, DMX, MIDI and proprietary control architectures. Remote and on-site diagnostics, optimisation and resolution for Dante audio networks, console integration, MADI and complex multi-protocol AV/IT environments, plus design, deployment and management of production IT and show networks (Windows Server, Active Directory, Cisco/Meraki switching, managed VLANs).
Expected outcome
Systems checked against the agreed scope before opening, and faults traced to a cause so the operating team knows where things stand.
Medialon Alcorn Beckhoff CANbus PLC SCADA DMX Dante L3 Q-SYS Cisco Meraki

Operational Handover & Documentation

When you need it
The installation team is leaving or an exhibition is moving venue, and the operating team needs what it takes to run the system without them.
What I provide
Operating and maintenance documentation, SOPs (preshow, show, shutdown, maintenance), network diagrams and commissioning checklists, plus coordination between the installation and technical teams and the teams who will operate the show. Full technical supervision and operations management for travelling and permanent immersive exhibitions, from pre-build verification through opening and ongoing daily operations, including daily operational procedures, staff rostering and show-move logistics.
Expected outcome
Documented procedures and technical records the operating team can work from, and knowledge that stays with the project.
SOPs Network Diagrams Commissioning Docs Operations Management

What is the difference between on-site contract roles and remote consulting? +

On-site contract roles involve physical attendance at the venue or production — exhibition technical supervision, show commissioning, or touring crew — with fees quoted on request, plus travel and accommodation. Remote consulting covers troubleshooting, system advisory, documentation, and AV network diagnostics via video call and written deliverables, also quoted on request.
