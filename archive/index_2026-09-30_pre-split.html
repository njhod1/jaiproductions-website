Profile

Nigel
Hodgson

Every department in a live show has a designer — lighting, sound, set. Automation and systems integration rarely gets the same recognition, but for over 35 years, that's been my role: translating artistic intent into technical scope across animatronic show control, multi-protocol AV networking, IT infrastructure, and technical operations management — and translating technical constraint back into creative options, so producers get the best achievable outcome rather than a forced compromise. Across touring productions, permanent installations, and travelling exhibitions on every continent.

I have served as Head of Show Control, Head of Creatures, Technical Supervisor, and IT Coordinator on productions that run seven days a week, move to a new venue every week, and cannot fail. That operating reality — not classroom theory — informs every engagement I take on.

Based between Sydney and Osaka, I am positioned across the Asia Pacific region and available for remote consultations, on-site commissioning support, and retained technical advisory for exhibition companies, production houses, and themed entertainment operators.

Case Studies

The projects below show what that translation looks like in practice — from holding multiple live international exhibition sites running simultaneously, to rebuilding a full touring show from scratch every seven days, to keeping a modern immersive production's network invisible to its audience.

01
Travelling Exhibition · USA, UK, Asia, Australia, Middle East · 2019–2021
Jurassic World: The Exhibition — Technical Supervisor Worldwide

Victory Hill Exhibitions / Cityneon Group. A major IP touring exhibition running seven days a week across multiple concurrent international venues — each with high guest throughput, strict uptime requirements, and a control architecture spanning Alcorn McBride, Medialon, Beckhoff, servo and hydraulic systems, PID motion control, Dante media distribution, multi-node AV networks, and Cisco IT infrastructure. Responsible for all technical operations, staff training, and daily show readiness across every global deployment simultaneously.

Outcome Multiple international venues opened on schedule. Consistent seven-day operation achieved with minimal downtime. Full SOP library developed covering preshow, show, shutdown, and maintenance. Local teams trained to operate independently. Troubleshooting time reduced through documentation and cross-domain diagnostic workflows across automation, AV, and IT.
02
Arena Tour · North America, Europe, Asia · 2007–2019
Walking With Dinosaurs — Head of Creatures / Head of Controls

Nick Grace Management / Global Creatures. Over a decade and multiple tour legs, responsible for all animatronic systems, mechanical reliability, controls, and daily show readiness on one of the world's largest touring arena productions. Full-size wireless animatronic dinosaurs operated via custom hydraulic and servo systems with PID loop control, BlackTrax live tracking, DMX, MIDI, Ethernet, and CANbus — across up to 12 shows per week with a full venue move every seven days. Cross-department coordination with lighting, audio, SFX, automation, and puppeteers was a constant operational requirement.

Outcome High animatronic reliability sustained across years of intensive touring. Downtime minimised through proactive mechanical inspection and preventative maintenance regimes. Artistic consistency maintained across vastly different venue configurations and regions. Operators and puppeteers trained to consistent performance standards at every stop.
03
Live Production · Australia · 2023–2024
Metaverse of Magic — Show Networking Engineer

Jones. An immersive live magic show combining LED panel systems, interactive media, NDI video streaming, and a gaming software environment served from an onsite server. Responsible for designing, supporting, and maintaining the entire networked media environment — including wired and wireless infrastructure, NDI distribution, and multi-node media delivery — in a high-RF live performance environment where wireless congestion and streaming stability were the critical risk factors.

Outcome Stable, high-quality NDI video streaming delivered throughout the production run. Network-related issues eliminated through proactive monitoring and redundancy strategies. LED, media server, and network systems integrated into a unified, show-reliable environment. Rapid on-the-floor troubleshooting during rehearsals prevented issues from reaching performance.
04
Musical Theatre · Australia · 2024–2025
Titanique — Head of Sound, Australian Premiere

Michael Cassel / JPJ Audio. Led the full sound department for the Australian premiere of Titanique from script edit through to opening night. System architecture: Yamaha console, Meyer managed speaker system, Dante audio distribution, QLab playback, and Shure wireless. The role required building the show from zero — developing all show files, cue stacks, and RF workflows in close collaboration with the sound designer, director, cast, and crew — while simultaneously training operators and running technical rehearsals.

Outcome Successful Australian premiere delivered with consistent audio quality across all performances. Show files, playback systems, and RF workflows built and stabilised from the ground up. Technical and creative teams aligned throughout the production process. Operators trained and confident by opening night.
