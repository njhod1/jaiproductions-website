# Content Guide — jaiproductions.com.au

## Core Positioning

**The single line that defines everything:**
> "The person who translates artistic intent into technical scope, and technical constraint back into creative options, across every department."

Every content update should be consistent with this positioning. Nigel is not a pure IT person, not a pure AV engineer, and not a pure production manager — he is the integrator who makes the creative vision technically achievable and makes technical realities creatively navigable.

---

## Tone and Voice

**What this site sounds like:**
- Direct and specific — every claim backed by a named production or technology
- Confident without boasting — the work speaks; the writing doesn't oversell
- Technical credibility — use correct terminology; this audience knows the difference
- Warm where appropriate — contact section and FAQ can be conversational
- No fluff — "passionate about", "dynamic", "results-driven" — never

**What this site does NOT sound like:**
- Generic consulting website language
- Corporate HR-speak
- Overly humble or hedging
- Salesy or promotional

**Test:** Would a technical director at Creature Technology Company or NEON read this and think "yes, this person knows what they're talking about"? If not, rewrite.

- Available now, for both on-site contract and remote consulting
- 35 years, real productions, every continent
- Credentials are specific and verifiable

### About / Profile

- Global experience is genuine, not aspirational

### Services
- Lead with animatronics and show control (highest value, most differentiated)
- AV network optimisation — not design, not architecture: optimisation and troubleshooting

### Case Studies
- Focus on the problem and outcome, not just the technology list
- Show the scale — concurrent venues, weekly moves, 10-hour days, 7 days a week
- The translator framing should be implicit in every case study

### Rates
- USD only
- Two tracks are deliberate — on-site contract is not the same as consulting
- Rates are market-competitive for the US themed entertainment market

### FAQ
- These feed the JSON-LD schema — answers should be specific and factual
- Japan/Osaka availability answer is important — Universal Studios Japan is in Osaka
- Do not make FAQ answers vague or generic
